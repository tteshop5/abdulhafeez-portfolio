Project Requirements

Clear responsibilities before development begins.

Some projects require third-party services or paid platforms. These costs are separate from development fees unless we agree otherwise in writing.

Client-Owned Services

Project-specific accounts and subscriptions should normally remain under the client's ownership.

  • Domain registration
  • Web hosting
  • Cloudflare paid plans, if required
  • GitHub paid plans, if required
  • Shopify plans

Additional Project Costs

Some websites may require third-party tools or premium services depending on the client's requirements.

  • Premium themes
  • Premium plugins or apps
  • Third-party APIs
  • Special software or subscriptions
  • Other required external services
Ownership principle

The client should own their domain, hosting, platform accounts and project-specific subscriptions whenever possible. This keeps the client's digital assets independent and prevents unnecessary recurring costs being placed on the developer.

Payment Terms

Simple and transparent project terms.

Payment arrangements are discussed and confirmed before development begins.

Depending on the project, payment may be arranged after completion or through an agreed milestone structure.

Development fees, project scope, delivery timeline and third-party expenses are clearly discussed before the project is confirmed.

Third-party services such as domains, hosting, premium software and platform subscriptions are separate from development fees unless specifically included in the agreed project scope.
