1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files or similar technologies that may be stored on your device when you visit a website. They can help websites remember information, maintain functionality, understand general website usage, or improve the user experience.

Cookies may be temporary and expire when you close your browser, or they may remain on your device for a longer period depending on their purpose and configuration.

2. Essential Technologies

Some technologies may be necessary for a website to operate correctly, securely, or reliably.

Depending on the hosting environment and technical services used by this website, essential technologies may support functions such as security, page delivery, basic navigation, or technical operation.

This website does not intentionally use cookies simply to collect personal information when they are not necessary for the website's operation.

3. Analytics Technologies

Analytics tools may be added to this website in the future to understand general website performance and visitor behavior.

If analytics services are implemented, they may collect information such as pages visited, general device or browser information, approximate geographic information, referral sources, and general usage patterns.

Analytics will be used for legitimate purposes such as improving website performance, content, usability, and user experience.

This policy does not mean that a specific analytics service is currently installed. Any future analytics implementation will be subject to the applicable service provider's terms and privacy practices.

4. Third-Party Services

Some website features or external links may involve third-party platforms. Depending on how the website is configured, these providers may use their own cookies or similar technologies.

Examples may include:

  • Website hosting and infrastructure providers
  • Cloudflare or similar security and delivery services
  • GitHub or similar development and hosting platforms
  • Professional networking platforms such as LinkedIn
  • Employment platforms such as Indeed
  • Video meeting services such as Zoom
  • Analytics or search services that may be added later

When you leave this website and visit a third-party website, that third party's own privacy and cookie policies apply.

5. Advertising and Marketing Cookies

Advertising or marketing technologies are not represented as currently active on this website unless they have actually been implemented.

If advertising services, including Google Ads or other advertising platforms, are added in the future, those services may use cookies or similar technologies for purposes such as measuring advertising performance, understanding conversions, or delivering relevant advertising.

Any future advertising implementation will be reviewed and reflected in the applicable privacy and cookie information.

6. Managing Cookies Through Your Browser

Most modern web browsers allow you to view, block, delete, or otherwise manage cookies through their settings.

You can usually find cookie controls in your browser's privacy, security, or site settings.

Blocking certain cookies may affect the functionality of some websites or features.

This Cookie Policy may be updated when the website's technology, services, analytics tools, advertising features, or legal requirements change.

The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
